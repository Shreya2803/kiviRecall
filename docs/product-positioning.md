# Product Positioning

Kivi hears every word a person dictates, in every app. That makes it the last part of
the system that should be trusted to profile the person doing the work — and the only
part positioned to remember the work itself, in full.

Semantic memory gives Kivi a durable record of that work — decisions, commitments,
ownership, project status, vocabulary — spread across weeks and applications. Not a
person's likeness.

Kivi remembers your work, not you. It shows everything it knows. It quotes the
dictation behind every answer. And it stays silent when it doesn't know.
